### article-product-visual-system
| 字段 | 中文修改稿 | English Draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 标题 | 从产品卖点到商业视觉：科技产品 KV 的组织方式 | From Product Benefits to Commercial Visuals: Structuring Technology Product KV |
| 分类 | 产品视觉 | Product Visual |
| 摘要 | 记录如何把硬件功能、应用场景和营销语言整理成可传播的产品主视觉。 | How hardware functions, use scenarios and marketing language can be organized into a clear product key visual. |
| 正文 | 科技产品视觉不能只停留在“好看”。更重要的是把用户能理解的卖点组织出来：核心功能是什么，使用场景在哪里，为什么值得相信。<br><br>我的做法通常是先拆产品功能，再找最有商业价值的场景，最后把图像、标题、功能 icon 和局部说明压缩成一个清晰的信息层级。 | Technology product visuals should go beyond looking attractive. The more important task is to organize benefits that users can understand: what the core function is, where it is used and why it is trustworthy.<br><br>My usual process is to break down product functions first, identify the most commercially valuable scenario, then compress imagery, headline, feature icons and detail notes into a clear information hierarchy. |

| --- | --- | --- |

| 分类 | 工作流 | Workflow |
